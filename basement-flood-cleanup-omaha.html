Flooded Basement? Safety Comes First.

  • Do not enter standing water if electrical equipment, outlets, or wiring may be affected.
  • Never operate electrical equipment or attempt to disconnect power while standing in water.
  • Avoid contact with sewage, contaminated floodwater, and unknown chemicals.
  • Keep children and pets away from affected areas.
  • If you suspect structural instability, electrical danger, or a gas leak, leave the affected area.
  • Contact emergency services at 911 if there is an immediate threat to life or safety.

Omaha Restoration™ is not an emergency response agency.

What Causes Basement Flooding?

Omaha-area properties can experience basement flooding for several reasons. Identifying the source of the water is an important part of determining the appropriate cleanup and repair.

Heavy Rain and Groundwater

Rainfall, poor drainage, and groundwater accumulation may allow water to enter through foundation openings or basement walls.

Burst and Leaking Pipes

Plumbing failures, frozen pipes, and leaking water supply lines can cause significant interior water damage.

Sump Pump Failure

Mechanical problems, power outages, and overwhelmed drainage systems can prevent a sump pump from removing accumulated water.

Sewer and Drain Backups

Sewer backups may introduce contaminated water into a property and require specialized assessment and cleanup procedures.

Property Insurance

Does Insurance Cover Basement Flooding?

Insurance coverage depends on the cause of the damage, your specific policy, applicable exclusions, deductibles, and other policy conditions.

Sudden and accidental plumbing damage may be covered under certain homeowners insurance policies. External flooding, groundwater intrusion, and sewer backups may require separate coverage or additional endorsements.

When safe, document visible damage and contact your insurance company to discuss your coverage and claim requirements.

Omaha Restoration™ does not determine insurance coverage, negotiate insurance settlements, or act as an insurance adjuster. No insurance approval or reimbursement is guaranteed.

Consumer Protection

Before Hiring a Restoration Contractor

Property owners should independently review contractor qualifications before authorizing work or making payments.

  • Verify applicable contractor registration and licensing requirements.
  • Request proof of appropriate insurance.
  • Obtain a written estimate and project agreement.
  • Confirm pricing, payment terms, and project scope.
  • Ask about warranties, permits, and expected completion schedules.

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly should basement water be removed?

Water damage should be addressed as soon as safely possible. Delays may increase damage and mold-related concerns. An independent restoration professional can assess your property's specific conditions.

Does Omaha Restoration™ perform cleanup?

No. Omaha Restoration™ operates as a contractor referral and connection platform. Physical cleanup, mitigation, construction, and repairs are performed by independent contractors.

How much does basement flood cleanup cost?

Costs depend on the amount of water, contamination, affected materials, equipment requirements, and the extent of property damage. Contractors independently determine their estimates and service pricing.

Is a contractor guaranteed to be available?

No. Contractor availability depends on location, current workloads, project requirements, weather, and other circumstances. Submitting a request does not guarantee an appointment or acceptance.

Who is responsible for the restoration work?

The independent contractor engaged to perform the work is responsible for its services, workmanship, applicable permits, and obligations under its agreement with the customer.

Can businesses and property managers submit requests?

Yes. Property owners, businesses, landlords, and property managers may submit contractor connection requests, subject to project suitability and contractor availability.
