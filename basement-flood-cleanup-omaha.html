Important Safety Information

Flooded Basement? Safety Comes First.

  • Do not enter standing water if electrical outlets, wiring, or equipment may be affected.
  • Never operate electrical equipment or attempt to disconnect power while standing in water.
  • Avoid contact with sewage, contaminated floodwater, and unknown chemicals.
  • Keep children and pets away from affected areas.
  • Leave the affected area if you suspect structural instability or electrical danger.
  • If you suspect a gas leak, leave the building and contact emergency services or your gas utility from a safe location.
  • Call 911 if there is an immediate threat to life or safety.

Omaha Restoration™ is not an emergency response agency.

Understanding Water Damage

What Causes Basement Flooding?

Omaha-area properties can experience basement flooding for several reasons. Identifying the source of the water is important when determining appropriate cleanup and repair needs.

Heavy Rain and Groundwater

Rainfall, poor drainage, and groundwater accumulation may allow water to enter through foundation openings or basement walls.

Burst and Leaking Pipes

Frozen pipes, plumbing failures, and leaking water supply lines can cause significant interior water damage.

Sump Pump Failure

Mechanical problems, power outages, and overwhelmed drainage systems can prevent a sump pump from removing accumulated water.

Sewer and Drain Backups

Sewer backups may introduce contaminated water into a property and require specialized assessment and cleanup.

Property Owner Guidance

What to Do After Your Basement Floods

  1. Protect yourself and others. Avoid unsafe areas and electrical hazards.
  2. Stop the water source when safe. If a plumbing failure is responsible, shut off an accessible water valve only if it can be reached safely.
  3. Document visible damage. Take photographs from a safe location and retain relevant records.
  4. Contact your insurance provider. Ask about coverage, documentation, and claim requirements.
  5. Arrange professional assessment. Hidden moisture and contaminated water may require specialized equipment and professional assistance.

Property Damage Prevention

Why Basement Water Damage Should Be Addressed Promptly

Water can remain trapped beneath flooring, behind walls, and inside insulation even after visible standing water has disappeared.

Persistent moisture may contribute to material deterioration, odors, and mold growth.

Drying water-damaged materials within 24 to 48 hours, when safely possible, can help reduce the risk of mold growth.

Do not operate fans or electrical drying equipment where electrical hazards or contaminated water may be present.

Additional information is available through the Environmental Protection Agency .

Property Insurance

Does Insurance Cover Basement Flooding?

Insurance coverage depends on the cause of the damage, your specific policy, applicable exclusions, deductibles, and other policy conditions.

Sudden and accidental plumbing damage may be covered under certain homeowners insurance policies.

External flooding, groundwater intrusion, and sewer backups may require separate coverage or additional endorsements.

When safe, document visible damage and contact your insurance company to discuss coverage and claim requirements.

Omaha Restoration™ does not determine insurance coverage, negotiate insurance settlements, or act as an insurance adjuster. No insurance approval or reimbursement is guaranteed.

Consumer Protection

Before Hiring a Restoration Contractor

Property owners should independently review contractor qualifications before authorizing work or making payments.
