Burst Pipe? Protect Yourself First.

Water from a damaged pipe may reach electrical equipment, appliances, ceilings, walls, and structural materials. Do not assume an affected area is safe.

  • Do not enter standing water if electrical outlets, wiring, or equipment may be affected.
  • Never touch electrical panels, switches, or power cords while standing in water.
  • Contact your electrical utility or a qualified electrician if electrical hazards may exist.
  • Stay away from sagging ceilings, damaged walls, and potentially unstable building materials.
  • Keep children and pets away from affected areas.
  • Call 911 if there is an immediate threat to life or safety.

Omaha Restoration™ is not an emergency response agency.

Property Owner Action Guide

What to Do When a Pipe Bursts

Taking appropriate action may help reduce additional property damage. Never attempt any step that could place you or others in danger.

  1. Stay Away From Unsafe Areas

    Avoid standing water, electrical hazards, damaged ceilings, and potentially unstable building materials.

  2. Shut Off the Water Supply When Safe

    If the damaged pipe is part of your plumbing system, shutting off an accessible water supply valve may help stop additional water from entering the property. Do not enter an unsafe area to reach the valve.

  3. Arrange Plumbing Assistance

    A qualified plumber may be needed to identify the damaged pipe, stop the leak, and determine the appropriate plumbing repair.

  4. Document Visible Damage

    When safe, photograph affected rooms, damaged belongings, and visible water damage. Keep relevant receipts and records.

  5. Notify Your Insurance Provider

    Contact your insurance company to discuss your policy, applicable coverage, documentation requirements, and appropriate mitigation procedures.

  6. Arrange Water Damage Assessment

    Water may remain behind walls, beneath flooring, and inside insulation after the visible leak has stopped. A qualified restoration professional can assess affected materials and potential drying needs.

Common Causes of Burst Pipes in Omaha

Nebraska's seasonal temperature changes and aging plumbing systems can contribute to pipe failures in residential and commercial properties.

Frozen Water Pipes

Freezing temperatures can cause water inside pipes to freeze and create pressure, potentially damaging pipes or connections.

Aging Plumbing Systems

Corrosion, deteriorating materials, worn connections, and previous damage may contribute to plumbing failures.

Excessive Water Pressure

Abnormal pressure within a plumbing system may place additional stress on pipes, fittings, and connections.

Insufficient Pipe Insulation

Pipes located near exterior walls, unheated garages, crawlspaces, and other cold areas may be more vulnerable to freezing temperatures.

Damaged Plumbing Connections

Failed fittings, damaged supply lines, deteriorated seals, and loose connections may release water into surrounding building materials.

Equipment and Appliance Failures

Water heaters, washing machines, plumbing fixtures, and connected supply lines can fail and cause substantial interior water damage.

Water Damage Prevention

Why Hidden Moisture Matters

Water released from a burst pipe may travel through walls, ceilings, flooring, and other building materials.

Even after the plumbing leak has stopped, trapped moisture may contribute to material deterioration, odors, and mold growth.

Mold may begin growing within 24 to 48 hours under favorable moisture conditions. Appropriate drying should begin as soon as safely possible.

Do not operate fans, household appliances, or electrical drying equipment in areas where electrical hazards or contaminated water may be present.

For additional information, visit the Environmental Protection Agency mold resources .

Property Insurance

Does Insurance Cover Burst Pipe Damage?

Certain homeowners and commercial property insurance policies may provide coverage for sudden and accidental water damage resulting from a burst pipe.

Coverage for the damaged pipe itself, resulting property damage, long-term leaks, frozen plumbing, and other circumstances depends on your specific insurance policy.

Coverage may also be affected by exclusions, deductibles, maintenance requirements, occupancy conditions, and other policy terms.

Contact your insurance company to determine your coverage and claim requirements. Keep photographs, written estimates, invoices, and relevant communications.

Omaha Restoration™ does not determine insurance coverage, negotiate insurance settlements, provide public adjusting services, or guarantee insurance approval or reimbursement.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do immediately after a pipe bursts?

Prioritize personal safety. Stay away from electrical hazards and unstable building materials. Shut off an accessible water supply valve only if it can be reached safely, and arrange appropriate professional assistance.

Can a frozen pipe burst after it thaws?

Freezing can damage a pipe or connection. Water may begin escaping when the ice thaws and normal water flow resumes. A suspected frozen or damaged pipe should be evaluated appropriately.

Does Omaha Restoration™ repair burst pipes?

No. Omaha Restoration™ operates as a contractor referral and connection platform. Plumbing repairs, restoration, and other physical services are performed by independent professionals.

Do I need a plumber or a restoration contractor?

A plumber may be needed to identify and repair the source of the leak. A restoration contractor may be needed to assess and address resulting water damage. Some projects require both.

How much does burst pipe cleanup cost?

Costs depend on the extent of water damage, affected materials, equipment requirements, labor, and the scope of repairs. Independent contractors determine their own estimates and pricing.
