1. Parties and Services

This Business Contractor Sourcing and Referral Agreement ("Agreement") is between Murrays Empire LLC, a Nebraska limited liability company operating under the brand Omaha Restoration™ ("Company"), and the business identified in this form ("Client").

Company provides independent contractor sourcing, introductions, referrals, and administrative coordination in exchange for agreed compensation.

Company does not directly perform physical construction, supervise job-site activities, administer payroll, supply employees, operate as an employer of record, or guarantee project completion.

Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship, legal partnership, joint venture, franchise, or agency between the parties. Neither party may bind the other without written authorization.

2. Pricing and Assignment Authorization

Standard sourcing is $150 per person for requests made at least 24 hours before the requested arrival. Rush sourcing is $300 per person for requests made less than 24 hours before the requested arrival. Rush pricing does not guarantee availability.

Fees are calculated individually for each authorized person, including multiple individuals from the same contracting company.

Recurring discounts require a separate written agreement establishing applicable rates, monthly commitment, billing period, and any shortfall adjustments.

Before each compensated assignment, Client must affirmatively approve the requested services, location, number of people, required qualifications, arrival requirements, applicable fees, paying party, and any cancellation or repeat-assignment terms.

Email, text message, or electronic acceptance may establish written authorization. Unauthorized additional personnel do not automatically generate additional charges. Silence does not constitute acceptance.

No undisclosed or retroactive compensation changes may be imposed. Assignment-specific pricing modifications require mutual written acceptance.

3. Payment Terms and Collections

The applicable sourcing fee becomes earned when an authorized individual arrives at the agreed location and is available and appropriately qualified to perform the requested services.

Earned fees and lawfully assessed cancellation charges are payable within five (5) business days after the applicable payment trigger, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Sourcing fees are independent of hours worked, contractor compensation, project revenue, subsequent construction payments, and project completion.

Payments must be made through an authorized business payment method designated by Company. Client shall identify the applicable invoice or assignment when submitting payment.

Client shall reasonably cooperate in confirming qualifying arrivals and promptly report material assignment discrepancies.

Overdue, undisputed amounts may accrue a non-compounding late charge of 1.5% per month, or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law, whichever is less.

Company may suspend additional sourcing services while undisputed balances remain overdue and pursue lawful collection remedies.

Reasonable collection expenses, court costs, and attorney fees may be recovered only to the extent permitted by applicable law or awarded by a court or arbitrator under an enforceable contractual provision.

Client shall provide written notice of invoice disputes within ten (10) business days after receiving the invoice, identifying the disputed amount, reason, and reasonably available supporting documentation.

Both parties shall attempt to resolve disputes in good faith. Undisputed amounts remain payable by their original deadlines. This notice requirement does not waive rights or defenses that cannot lawfully be waived.

4. Independent Contractor Compensation

Hourly rates, daily rates, project pricing, construction scope, and payment arrangements are negotiated independently between Client and the service provider.

Company does not establish or administer those payments and is not responsible for paying the service provider's labor, wages, materials, equipment, or other independently negotiated expenses.

Actual worker classification depends on applicable law and the circumstances of the working relationship, not contractual labels alone.

Client and each service provider remain responsible for their respective lawful tax, insurance, employment, and contractual obligations.

5. Cancellations and Replacements

When accepted in the assignment authorization, Client cancellation before confirmed travel begins incurs no fee.

Client cancellation after confirmed travel begins may incur 50% of the authorized sourcing fee, to the extent reasonably related to anticipated losses and legally enforceable.

Cancellation after qualifying arrival does not eliminate the earned sourcing fee.

Except for an applicable Client-initiated cancellation charge, no sourcing fee is owed for an individual who cancels, fails to arrive, or fails to satisfy the agreed arrival and qualification requirements.

Replacement efforts are subject to availability and do not create duplicate sourcing charges. Unearned payments and billing errors shall be refunded or corrected.

Company shall not collect both a full sourcing fee and a separate cancellation fee for the same individual assignment.

6. Non-Circumvention and Repeat Assignments

For twelve (12) months following a documented introduction, Client shall not intentionally bypass Company to avoid expressly agreed sourcing fees for qualifying assignments.

Client shall not knowingly use an affiliated business, intermediary, subcontractor, alternative payment arrangement, or false assignment information to conceal qualifying work or evade lawfully agreed fees.

New assignments involving introduced service providers may generate additional fees only where repeat-assignment obligations, scope, pricing, duration, and payment triggers were expressly accepted in writing.

Additional visits necessary to complete the original assignment do not automatically generate new fees.

This provision does not prohibit legitimate direct communication, lawful worker mobility, or independently established business relationships.

Client shall promptly disclose relevant pre-existing relationships concerning the same assignment and report qualifying repeat assignments within two (2) business days.

Intentional circumvention constitutes a material breach. Company may pursue lawfully recoverable unpaid fees and proven damages without imposing unauthorized penalties.

Applicable non-circumvention obligations survive termination for the remainder of the original twelve-month period.

7. Separate Compensation Agreements

Company may maintain separate compensation agreements with independent contracting businesses.

Compensation from both Client and a service provider shall not be collected unless legally permitted, disclosed to the affected parties, and affirmatively accepted before the compensated introduction.

Company may reduce or waive fees through written authorization.

Client is not responsible for another party's separate compensation obligations unless expressly agreed in writing.

No compensation arrangement authorizes prohibited employment-placement fees, unlawful wage deductions, or undisclosed duplicate charges.

8. Responsibilities and Qualifications

Service providers must possess applicable registrations, licenses, insurance, and qualifications required by law and the accepted assignment.

Company shall not knowingly represent unverified qualifications as verified or authorize independent assignments before applicable credential requirements have been satisfied.

Client shall independently evaluate service providers, provide accurate project information, coordinate property access, disclose known safety concerns, and comply with applicable workplace and construction requirements.

Client and service providers are responsible for independently negotiated construction contracts, permits, supervision, safety, workmanship, warranties, and project performance according to their respective legal and contractual obligations.

Company does not guarantee continuous availability, workmanship, construction pricing, insurance coverage, or project completion.

Company may decline or suspend assignments involving unsafe conditions, unlawful activities, inaccurate project information, or unresolved qualification concerns.

9. Liability, Indemnification and Legal Compliance

Each party remains responsible for its own acts, omissions, negligence, misconduct, and legal obligations.

Company does not assume construction obligations independently undertaken by Client or service providers.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, Client shall indemnify and hold harmless Murrays Empire LLC, Omaha Restoration™, and their respective owners, members, managers, and authorized representatives against third-party claims, damages, liabilities, and reasonable defense expenses to the extent caused by:

  • Client's negligent acts or omissions.
  • Client's material contractual breaches.
  • Client's unlawful conduct or misconduct.
  • Unsafe conditions for which Client is legally responsible.
  • Acts of Client's personnel for which Client is legally responsible.
  • Client's failure to satisfy applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

This provision does not require Client to indemnify Company for Company's own negligence, misconduct, or other liability where prohibited by applicable law.

Company shall provide reasonably prompt notice of an indemnifiable claim and reasonably cooperate in its resolution. Neither party may impose a settlement obligation on the other without written consent.

Activities requiring contractor registration, employment-agency licensing, trade licensing, insurance, or other governmental authorization shall not proceed until applicable requirements have been satisfied.

No prohibited employment-placement fees, unlawful wage deductions, worker misclassification, fraudulent insurance activities, or other unlawful compensation arrangements are authorized.

Company does not negotiate or adjust insurance claims.

10. Confidentiality and Business Records

Both parties shall reasonably protect confidential business, customer, property, and contractor information and use it only for legitimate business, contractual, or legal purposes.

Neither party may improperly sell, disclose, distribute, or misuse confidential information received through the sourcing relationship.

Client may not misrepresent its relationship with Company, claim unauthorized authority, or use Company trademarks or branding without written permission.

Relevant assignment authorizations, arrival confirmations, invoices, payments, and agreement records shall be retained for at least three (3) years, or longer when required by law or an existing dispute.

Upon reasonable written request, Client shall provide relevant documentation necessary to verify authorized assignments, qualifying arrivals, and applicable sourcing compensation.

Client consents to transmission of submitted business information and electronic signatures through a third-party form processing service for agreement administration and recordkeeping.

Applicable confidentiality and recordkeeping obligations survive termination.

11. Termination and Binding Arbitration

This Agreement is non-exclusive and does not guarantee a minimum number of assignments. No automatic monthly subscription or annual renewal commitment is established.

Either party may terminate this Agreement upon fifteen (15) calendar days' written notice.

Company may suspend services or terminate immediately upon written notice for fraud, unlawful conduct, serious safety concerns, intentional circumvention, material nonpayment, or an incurable material breach.

Termination does not eliminate lawfully earned fees, outstanding payment obligations, or applicable surviving confidentiality, indemnification, non-circumvention, recordkeeping, and dispute-resolution provisions.

This Agreement is governed by Nebraska law, subject to mandatory applicable laws in other jurisdictions.

Before commencing arbitration, the complaining party shall provide written notice describing the dispute and requested remedy. The parties shall attempt good-faith informal resolution for fifteen (15) calendar days, unless urgent legal relief is necessary.

Except for eligible small-claims matters, disputes arising from or relating to this Agreement shall be resolved through binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association under its applicable Commercial Arbitration Rules.

Arbitration shall take place in Douglas County, Nebraska, unless the parties agree otherwise in writing or mandatory applicable law requires another location.

Unless applicable rules require otherwise, one neutral arbitrator shall decide the dispute.

Filing fees, administrative expenses, and arbitrator compensation shall initially be allocated according to applicable arbitration rules and mandatory law.

The arbitrator may award costs and attorney fees only when authorized by applicable law or an enforceable contractual provision.

Either party may instead bring an eligible claim in a court of competent small-claims jurisdiction.

Either party may seek temporary judicial relief where legally available without waiving applicable arbitration obligations. A court of competent jurisdiction may enter judgment upon an arbitration award.

By accepting this Agreement, the parties acknowledge that covered disputes will generally be resolved by an arbitrator rather than through a court trial or jury trial.

Business days exclude Saturdays, Sundays, and observed United States federal holidays.

The individual signing represents that they possess authority to bind the identified business and voluntarily consent to electronic signatures and electronic records.

Submission constitutes Client's offer to enter this Agreement. The Agreement becomes effective upon written acceptance by an authorized representative of Murrays Empire LLC.

Submission alone does not guarantee approval or establish an individual sourcing charge.

This Agreement, accepted assignment authorizations, and mutually accepted written amendments establish the commercial sourcing relationship.

Assignment-specific written terms control expressly identified assignment details. Other provisions remain effective unless expressly modified through mutual written acceptance.

Material changes require mutual written acceptance. Neither party may unilaterally impose retroactive compensation changes.

Unenforceable provisions shall be severed to the extent permitted by law. Failure to enforce a provision does not automatically waive future enforcement.

Neither party may transfer this Agreement to an unrelated third party without the other party's written consent, except where otherwise permitted by law.

Required notices may be delivered through designated business email addresses or another documented written method.

Both parties shall receive or have access to a copy of the mutually accepted Agreement. Official notices to Company may be sent to murraysempirellc@gmail.com.
