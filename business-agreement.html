Commercial Agreement Terms

1. Identification of Parties

This Business Contractor Sourcing & Referral Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Murrays Empire LLC, a Nebraska limited liability company operating as Omaha Restoration™ ("Omaha Restoration"), and the business identified in the accompanying registration information ("Client").

In consideration of the mutual promises, authorized sourcing services, and compensation obligations established herein, the Parties agree to the following terms.

2. Scope of Services

Omaha Restoration provides independent contractor identification, sourcing, referral, introduction, and administrative coordination services to businesses requiring construction, restoration, repair, maintenance, and other lawful services.

Omaha Restoration does not physically perform construction, administer payroll, establish labor rates, supervise construction personnel, or undertake responsibility for construction methods or project completion.

Omaha Restoration does not act as the employer of referred individuals under this Agreement. Actual legal classification shall be determined by applicable law and the circumstances of each working relationship.

3. Standard Sourcing Fee

The standard sourcing fee is one hundred fifty dollars ($150) per authorized individual person successfully sourced for an assignment, subject to the payment trigger established in this Agreement.

Standard sourcing applies to requests submitted at least twenty-four (24) hours before the requested arrival time, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

4. Rush Sourcing Fee

The rush sourcing fee is three hundred dollars ($300) per authorized individual person successfully sourced for a rush assignment, subject to the payment trigger established in this Agreement.

Rush sourcing applies to requests requiring arrival in less than twenty-four (24) hours, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Rush pricing reflects expedited sourcing efforts and does not guarantee contractor availability or arrival within a particular timeframe.

5. Recurring Business Pricing

Clients establishing approved written monthly volume commitments may qualify for the following sourcing rates:

Monthly Volume Standard Per Person Rush Per Person
1–4 $150 $300

Discounted pricing requires separate written approval by Omaha Restoration and affirmative acceptance by Client.

The applicable billing period, minimum volume, qualifying placements, shortfall adjustments, and billing arrangements must be established in the written recurring pricing authorization.

Estimated monthly volume provided during registration does not automatically establish a discount or minimum purchase obligation.

Standard pricing applies unless an alternative pricing arrangement has been accepted in writing.

6. Per-Person Sourcing Fees

Sourcing fees are calculated per individual person specifically requested, authorized, and successfully sourced through Omaha Restoration, including individuals affiliated with the same independent contracting business.

When Client authorizes multiple individuals, the applicable sourcing fee shall be calculated separately for each qualifying individual.

For example, five authorized individuals successfully sourced at the regular standard rate of $150 per person result in a total sourcing fee of $750.

Five authorized individuals successfully sourced at the regular rush rate of $300 per person result in a total sourcing fee of $1,500.

Additional individuals not requested or authorized by Client shall not automatically generate additional sourcing fees.

Per-person pricing applies only where the underlying sourcing arrangement and compensation structure are permitted by applicable law.

7. Independent Compensation Arrangements

All hourly rates, daily rates, project pricing, labor compensation, material charges, payment schedules, and construction terms shall be independently negotiated between Client and the applicable service provider.

Omaha Restoration shall not establish, administer, or control compensation payable by Client to service providers.

Omaha Restoration's sourcing fees are separate from the compensation independently negotiated between Client and service providers.

Client's sourcing fees are not calculated using project revenue, hourly compensation, profit margins, or total hours worked.

8. Individual Project Authorization

Each compensated assignment requires affirmative written authorization before the applicable introduction or dispatch.

The authorization shall establish:

  • Project or assignment reference.
  • Project location.
  • Requested services and qualifications.
  • Number of authorized individuals.
  • Standard or rush classification.
  • Applicable per-person sourcing fee.
  • Responsible paying party.
  • Requested arrival date and time.
  • Applicable cancellation terms.
  • Any agreed repeat-assignment obligations.

Written authorization may be established through email, text message, electronic signature, or another documented method of affirmative acceptance.

Silence or failure to respond does not constitute acceptance of new compensation obligations.

9. Payment Trigger

A sourcing fee becomes earned when an authorized individual arrives at the agreed location, available and appropriately qualified to perform the requested services.

Arrival may be documented through Client confirmation, service provider confirmation, written communications, or other reliable evidence.

A sourcing fee does not become earned merely because contact information was exchanged or an individual was identified.

Once the applicable sourcing fee has been lawfully earned, payment is not dependent upon project revenue, hours worked, subsequent construction payments, or project completion.

10. Payment Deadline

Earned sourcing fees are payable within five (5) business days following the applicable payment trigger, unless a different deadline has been mutually accepted in writing.

Omaha Restoration may issue electronic invoices identifying the assignment, applicable fees, payment deadline, and authorized personnel.

Client shall not withhold undisputed sourcing compensation solely because of an unrelated construction payment dispute.

11. Cancellation Policy

When expressly accepted in the applicable Project Authorization, the following cancellation terms shall apply:

  • Cancellation before confirmed travel begins: No sourcing or cancellation fee.
  • Client cancellation after an authorized individual begins confirmed travel: 50% of the applicable sourcing fee, subject to applicable law.
  • Client cancellation after qualifying arrival: 100% of the applicable earned sourcing fee.
  • Service provider cancellation or failure to arrive: No sourcing fee for that individual.

Travel-based cancellation charges must be reasonably related to legitimate sourcing efforts and anticipated losses and shall not operate as an unlawful penalty.

Cancellation charges shall not be duplicated with a full sourcing fee for the same individual and assignment.

12. Replacement Services

Omaha Restoration may attempt to identify a replacement when an authorized individual cancels or fails to arrive.

A replacement fulfilling the same original request shall not generate a duplicate sourcing fee.

The ordinary authorized sourcing fee becomes payable if the replacement satisfies the applicable payment trigger.

Replacement availability and arrival times are not guaranteed.

13. Compensation From Other Parties

Omaha Restoration may maintain separate commercial agreements with independent contractors and contracting businesses.

Omaha Restoration may establish Client-funded, Contractor-funded, or dual-funded compensation arrangements where legally permitted.

Compensation from multiple parties shall not be collected unless the arrangement is permitted by applicable law, disclosed to affected parties, and affirmatively accepted before the compensated introduction.

Client is not responsible for a service provider's separate compensation obligations unless expressly accepted in writing.

Omaha Restoration reserves the right to reduce or waive applicable fees through written authorization.

Nothing in this Agreement authorizes collection of prohibited employment-placement fees, unlawful deductions from wages, or other compensation prohibited by applicable law.

14. Non-Circumvention

For twelve (12) months following a documented introduction, Client shall not intentionally circumvent Omaha Restoration for the purpose of avoiding sourcing compensation expressly agreed upon for qualifying assignments.

This provision is limited to introduced service providers, qualifying opportunities, and applicable accepted compensation obligations.

Client shall not knowingly use affiliated entities, intermediaries, or alternative contracting arrangements to conceal qualifying assignments or evade agreed sourcing fees.

This provision does not prohibit direct communication, ordinary construction negotiations, lawful competition, or unrelated independently established business relationships.

This provision shall not restrict lawful employment opportunities or worker mobility in violation of applicable law.

15. Repeat Assignments

New assignments involving previously introduced service providers within twelve (12) months following the documented introduction may generate additional sourcing fees when the applicable repeat-assignment obligations were expressly accepted in writing.

The applicable repeat-assignment authorization must identify the qualifying scope, fee, duration, and payment trigger.

Additional visits, extended workdays, or continued performance required to complete the original authorized assignment shall not automatically generate additional sourcing fees.

Client shall report qualifying repeat assignments within two business days after becoming aware of the applicable assignment.

16. Independent Contractor Relationships

Service providers introduced through Omaha Restoration are intended to operate as independent third-party contractors or contracting businesses.

Client and service providers shall independently establish their contractual relationship and applicable compensation arrangements.

Actual employment or independent contractor classification shall be determined by applicable law and the circumstances of the working relationship, not solely by contractual labels.

Nothing in this Agreement authorizes worker misclassification, unlawful staffing activities, or avoidance of employment obligations.

17. Contractor Qualifications

Service providers must possess applicable registrations, licenses, insurance, and qualifications required by law or the accepted assignment.

Omaha Restoration may review available credential documentation and shall not knowingly represent unverified credentials as verified.

Credential reviews reflect information reasonably available at the time of review and do not constitute a continuing guarantee of qualifications, insurance coverage, or regulatory compliance.

Client shall independently evaluate service providers before authorizing physical work.

18. Client Responsibilities

Client shall provide accurate project information, disclose known safety concerns, coordinate property access, and independently approve construction arrangements.

Client represents that it possesses appropriate authority to request services for the identified property or project.

Client shall comply with applicable workplace, employment, safety, construction, insurance, and payment obligations arising from its relationship with service providers.

Client shall not represent Omaha Restoration as the employer, construction contractor, or payroll administrator when that representation is inaccurate.

19. No Guarantee of Availability

Omaha Restoration does not guarantee continuous availability, minimum personnel volume, contractor acceptance, response times, construction pricing, workmanship, insurance coverage, or project completion.

Each request remains subject to service provider availability, applicable qualifications, regulatory compliance, and acceptance of the assignment.

20. Confidentiality and Information Protection

Both Parties shall reasonably protect confidential business, customer, property, contractor, and operational information obtained through the commercial relationship.

Information may be used or disclosed when reasonably necessary to facilitate authorized services, administer compensation, obtain professional advice, comply with legal obligations, or enforce contractual rights.

Each Party shall maintain reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information involved.

21. Payment Disputes and Collections

Client shall identify legitimate invoice disputes in writing within five business days after receiving the invoice, where reasonably practicable.

Undisputed amounts remain payable by their applicable deadlines.

Omaha Restoration may suspend future sourcing services while undisputed compensation remains overdue and may pursue lawful collection remedies.

Attorney's fees, collection expenses, interest, and other charges may be recovered only to the extent authorized by applicable law or a separately enforceable written agreement.

22. Responsibility and Liability

Each Party remains responsible for its own acts, omissions, negligence, misconduct, and legal obligations.

Omaha Restoration does not assume responsibility for construction obligations independently undertaken by Client or service providers.

Construction methods, workmanship, equipment, materials, permits, site supervision, warranties, and job-site safety remain the responsibility of the appropriate parties under applicable law and their separate contractual arrangements.

Nothing in this Agreement eliminates responsibility for Omaha Restoration's own legally actionable conduct.

23. Regulatory Compliance

All services and compensation arrangements are subject to applicable federal, Nebraska, and local laws.

Activities requiring contractor registration, employment-agency licensing, staffing authorization, trade licensing, insurance, or other governmental authorization shall not proceed until applicable requirements have been satisfied.

Nothing in this Agreement authorizes unlicensed regulated work, worker misclassification, fraudulent insurance activities, unlawful referral compensation, or avoidance of required registrations.

Compensation prohibited by applicable law shall not be charged or collected.

24. Non-Exclusivity

Neither Party is required to maintain an exclusive commercial relationship.

Client may obtain services through other lawful sources, and Omaha Restoration may provide sourcing services to other businesses and property owners.

No minimum number of assignments is guaranteed unless separately agreed in writing.

25. Duration and Termination

Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon fifteen (15) calendar days' written notice.

Either Party may immediately suspend or terminate services in response to material fraud, unlawful conduct, serious safety concerns, or material breaches that cannot reasonably be cured.

Termination does not eliminate lawfully earned compensation or applicable obligations established before termination.

Applicable payment, confidentiality, non-circumvention, repeat-assignment, and dispute-resolution obligations survive termination for their agreed duration.

26. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Nebraska.

Before initiating formal proceedings, the Parties shall make a reasonable good-faith effort to resolve disputes through written communication.

Unless urgent legal relief is necessary, the Parties shall allow fifteen calendar days following written dispute notice for attempted resolution.

Unresolved disputes shall be submitted to a court of competent jurisdiction serving Douglas County, Nebraska, subject to applicable law.

Nothing prevents either Party from pursuing lawful small-claims remedies or emergency legal relief.

27. Amendments and Pricing Changes

Material changes to this Agreement require affirmative written acceptance by both Parties.

Omaha Restoration may propose updated pricing for future assignments, subject to Client's affirmative acceptance.

Previously accepted compensation obligations shall not be retroactively modified without mutual written agreement.

28. Electronic Signatures and Records

Client consents to electronic transactions and electronic records.

Electronic signatures and affirmative electronic acceptance may have the same legal effect as handwritten signatures to the extent permitted by applicable law.

Omaha Restoration may retain electronic records documenting signatory information, agreement versions, acceptance timestamps, Project Authorizations, and relevant communications.

Each Party shall be provided access to or a copy of the mutually accepted Agreement.

29. Authorized Representative

The individual electronically signing this Agreement represents that they possess sufficient authority to bind the identified business.

The signatory acknowledges having reviewed the Agreement and having had an opportunity to obtain independent legal advice.

30. Effective Date and Mutual Acceptance

Submission of this electronically signed Agreement constitutes Client's offer to enter into the commercial relationship under the stated terms.

This Agreement becomes effective upon written acceptance by an authorized representative of Murrays Empire LLC.

Submission alone does not constitute automatic approval, guaranteed contractor availability, or acceptance by Omaha Restoration.

Individual assignment fees require separate affirmative written authorization.

31. Business Day Definition

Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays observed in the United States.

32. Entire Agreement

This Agreement, accepted Project Authorizations, approved recurring pricing arrangements, and mutually accepted written amendments establish the commercial sourcing relationship between the Parties.

Separate contractor agreements remain independent unless expressly modified in writing.

An accepted Project Authorization controls conflicting general terms only for the specific assignment and provisions expressly identified.

33. Arrival and Assignment Verification

Client shall reasonably cooperate in confirming the arrival of authorized individuals and identifying any individuals who failed to arrive or did not satisfy the agreed assignment requirements.

Client shall report material arrival disputes promptly and, where reasonably practicable, within two business days.

Omaha Restoration may request relevant assignment records necessary to verify qualifying placements and earned sourcing fees.

This provision does not authorize unrestricted access to Client's payroll, financial records, or unrelated construction revenue.

34. Pre-Existing Relationships

Client does not owe a sourcing fee solely because Omaha Restoration identifies a service provider with whom Client already maintains an independently established relationship concerning the same assignment.

Client shall promptly disclose relevant pre-existing relationships after receiving the service provider's identity.

A separately requested and accepted sourcing service involving a previously known provider may generate an agreed fee where legally permitted.

35. Refunds and Billing Adjustments

Amounts paid for sourcing fees that have not become earned shall be refunded unless a separately accepted lawful cancellation charge or other authorized fee applies.

Earned sourcing fees are not automatically refundable because of a subsequent construction dispute or workmanship complaint unrelated to Omaha Restoration's own obligations.

Duplicate charges, billing errors, and improperly assessed fees shall be corrected.

Nothing in this provision eliminates lawful remedies for breach or misrepresentation.

36. Insurance-Funded Projects

Omaha Restoration does not prepare, submit, negotiate, interpret, or adjust insurance claims or determine insurance settlement amounts.

Sourcing compensation represents payment for legitimate sourcing services and is not compensation for insurance claim recovery or settlement negotiation.

Neither Party shall knowingly involve the other in fraudulent billing, unlawful claim inflation, unauthorized adjusting, or other unlawful activities.

37. No Unauthorized Commitments

Neither Party shall make unauthorized promises, warranties, representations, or contractual commitments on behalf of the other Party.

Omaha Restoration shall not make unauthorized construction commitments on behalf of Client or any service provider.

38. Record Retention

Each Party shall retain reasonably available records relating to accepted assignments, sourcing fees, arrival confirmations, cancellations, and payments for three years following the applicable assignment.

Records shall be retained longer when required by applicable law or reasonably necessary for an existing dispute.

39. Force Majeure

Neither Party shall be responsible for delays in performing obligations other than earned payment obligations caused by circumstances reasonably beyond its control.

The affected Party shall provide reasonable notice and resume performance when reasonably practicable.

This provision does not excuse payment of compensation already lawfully earned.

40. Official Notices

Official notices may be delivered by email, written correspondence, or another mutually accepted electronic method providing a reasonable record of delivery.

41. Severability and No Waiver

If a provision is determined to be unlawful or unenforceable, remaining provisions shall remain effective to the extent permitted by law.

Failure to enforce a provision on one occasion does not permanently waive that provision or other contractual rights.
