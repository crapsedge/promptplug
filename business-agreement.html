Agreement Terms

1. Parties and Services

This Agreement is between Murrays Empire LLC, a Nebraska limited liability company operating as Omaha Restoration™ ("Omaha Restoration"), and the business identified above ("Client"). In exchange for agreed sourcing services and compensation, both parties accept these terms.

Omaha Restoration provides independent contractor sourcing, introductions, referrals, and administrative coordination. Omaha Restoration does not perform physical construction, supervise job-site activities, administer payroll, or guarantee project completion.

2. Pricing and Assignment Authorization

Standard sourcing is $150 per person for requests made at least 24 hours before the requested arrival. Rush sourcing is $300 per person for requests made less than 24 hours before the requested arrival. Rush pricing does not guarantee availability.

Recurring discounts require a separate written agreement establishing the applicable rates, monthly commitment, billing period, and any shortfall adjustments.

Before each compensated assignment, Client must affirmatively approve the requested services, location, number of people, required qualifications, arrival requirements, applicable fees, paying party, and any cancellation or repeat-assignment terms. Email, text message, or electronic acceptance may establish written authorization.

Unauthorized additional personnel do not automatically generate additional charges. Silence does not constitute acceptance.

3. Payment Terms

The applicable sourcing fee becomes earned when an authorized individual arrives at the agreed location and is available and appropriately qualified to perform the requested services.

Earned fees and lawfully assessed cancellation charges are payable within five business days after the applicable payment trigger, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Sourcing fees are independent of hours worked, contractor compensation, project revenue, subsequent construction payments, and project completion.

Client shall reasonably cooperate in confirming qualifying arrivals and promptly report disputes. Omaha Restoration may suspend future services while undisputed amounts remain overdue and pursue lawful collection remedies.

4. Independent Contractor Compensation

Hourly rates, daily rates, project pricing, construction scope, and payment arrangements are negotiated independently between Client and the service provider.

Omaha Restoration does not establish or administer those payments. Actual worker classification depends on applicable law and the circumstances of the working relationship, not contractual labels alone.

5. Cancellations and Replacements

When accepted in the assignment authorization, cancellation before confirmed travel begins incurs no fee. Client cancellation after confirmed travel begins may incur 50% of the authorized sourcing fee, to the extent reasonably related to anticipated losses and legally enforceable. Cancellation after qualifying arrival does not eliminate the earned sourcing fee.

No sourcing fee is owed for an individual who cancels, fails to arrive, or fails to satisfy the agreed arrival and qualification requirements. Replacement efforts are subject to availability and do not create duplicate sourcing charges. Unearned payments and billing errors shall be refunded or corrected.

6. Non-Circumvention and Repeat Assignments

For twelve months following a documented introduction, Client shall not intentionally bypass Omaha Restoration to avoid expressly agreed sourcing fees for qualifying assignments.

New assignments involving introduced service providers may generate additional fees only where repeat-assignment obligations, scope, pricing, duration, and payment triggers were expressly accepted in writing.

Additional visits necessary to complete the original assignment do not automatically generate new fees. This provision does not prohibit direct communication, lawful worker mobility, or independently established business relationships.

Client shall promptly disclose relevant pre-existing relationships concerning the same assignment and report qualifying repeat assignments within two business days.

7. Separate Compensation Agreements

Omaha Restoration may maintain separate compensation agreements with independent contracting businesses.

Compensation from both Client and a service provider shall not be collected unless legally permitted, disclosed to the affected parties, and affirmatively accepted before the compensated introduction.

Omaha Restoration may reduce or waive fees through written authorization. Client is not responsible for another party's separate compensation obligations unless expressly agreed in writing.

8. Responsibilities and Qualifications

Service providers must possess the applicable registrations, licenses, insurance, and qualifications required by law and the accepted assignment.

Omaha Restoration shall not knowingly represent unverified qualifications as verified. Client shall independently evaluate service providers, provide accurate project information, coordinate access, disclose known safety concerns, and comply with applicable workplace and construction requirements.

Omaha Restoration does not guarantee continuous availability, workmanship, construction pricing, insurance coverage, or project completion. Neither party may make unauthorized commitments on behalf of the other.

9. Liability and Legal Compliance

Each party remains responsible for its own acts, omissions, negligence, misconduct, and legal obligations. Omaha Restoration does not assume construction obligations independently undertaken by Client or service providers.

Activities requiring contractor registration, employment-agency licensing, trade licensing, insurance, or other governmental authorization shall not proceed until applicable requirements have been satisfied.

No prohibited employment-placement fees, unlawful wage deductions, worker misclassification, fraudulent insurance activities, or other unlawful compensation arrangements are authorized. Omaha Restoration does not negotiate or adjust insurance claims.

10. Confidentiality and Business Records

Both parties shall reasonably protect confidential business, customer, property, and contractor information and use it only for legitimate business, contractual, or legal purposes.

Relevant assignment authorizations, arrival confirmations, invoices, payments, and agreement records shall be retained for at least three years, or longer when required by law or an existing dispute.

Client consents to transmission of submitted business information and electronic signatures through a third-party form processing service for agreement administration and recordkeeping.

11. Termination and Disputes

This Agreement is non-exclusive and does not guarantee a minimum number of assignments. Either party may terminate upon fifteen calendar days' written notice, or immediately suspend services for unlawful conduct, material fraud, serious safety concerns, or incurable material breach.

Termination does not eliminate lawfully earned fees or applicable surviving obligations. Nebraska law governs this Agreement. The parties shall first attempt good-faith written resolution of disputes for fifteen calendar days, unless urgent legal relief is necessary.

Unresolved disputes may be brought before a court of competent jurisdiction serving Douglas County, Nebraska, subject to applicable law. Business days exclude weekends and observed United States federal holidays.

12. Electronic Acceptance and Entire Agreement

The individual signing represents that they possess authority to bind the identified business and voluntarily consent to electronic signatures and records.

Submission constitutes Client's offer to enter this Agreement. The Agreement becomes effective upon written acceptance by an authorized representative of Murrays Empire LLC. Submission alone does not guarantee approval or establish an individual sourcing charge.

This Agreement, accepted assignment authorizations, and mutually accepted written amendments establish the commercial sourcing relationship. Material changes require mutual written acceptance. Unenforceable provisions shall be severed to the extent permitted by law.

Both parties shall receive or have access to a copy of the mutually accepted Agreement. Official notices to Omaha Restoration may be sent to murraysempirellc@gmail.com.
