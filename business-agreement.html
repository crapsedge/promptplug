Business Contractor Sourcing & Referral Agreement

This Agreement establishes the terms of a commercial relationship between Murrays Empire LLC, operating as Omaha Restoration™, and the business identified below.

This Agreement establishes the general terms of the business relationship. Individual project compensation must be separately accepted in writing before a compensated contractor introduction.

Authorized Business Representative

Commercial Agreement Terms

1. Identification of Parties

This Business Contractor Sourcing & Referral Agreement ("Agreement") is between Murrays Empire LLC, a Nebraska limited liability company operating under the brand Omaha Restoration™ ("Omaha Restoration"), and the business identified in the accompanying registration information ("Client").

The Parties intend to establish legally binding obligations upon mutual acceptance of this Agreement.

In consideration of the mutual promises, sourcing services, and compensation obligations established herein and through accepted Project Authorizations, the Parties agree as follows.

2. Purpose of Agreement

Omaha Restoration may provide independent contractor identification, sourcing, referral, and introduction services to Client for property-related, construction, restoration, maintenance, repair, and other lawful service opportunities.

This Agreement establishes an ongoing commercial relationship without requiring either Party to request, provide, or accept a minimum number of assignments.

3. Scope and Limitations of Services

Omaha Restoration provides referral and sourcing services and does not undertake responsibility for performing, managing, supervising, or completing physical construction, restoration, repair, maintenance, or regulated trade work.

Omaha Restoration does not:

  • Supply construction employees to Client.
  • Supervise construction personnel or job sites.
  • Determine construction methods or labor rates.
  • Establish Contractor construction pricing.
  • Guarantee construction completion.
  • Collect construction payments on behalf of Contractors.
  • Prepare, negotiate, submit, or adjust insurance claims.

Nothing in this Agreement exempts either Party from applicable registration, licensing, insurance, or regulatory requirements.

4. Independent Contractor Relationships

Contractors introduced through Omaha Restoration are independent third-party service providers.

An introduction does not, by itself, establish an employment, agency, partnership, joint venture, fiduciary, or subcontracting relationship with Omaha Restoration.

Client and Contractor shall independently establish their construction-related contractual relationship.

Construction pricing, scope, scheduling, materials, equipment, permits, workmanship, warranties, job-site safety, insurance, and construction payments remain the responsibility of the appropriate parties under their separate agreements and applicable law.

5. Client Responsibilities

Client shall provide accurate and reasonably complete information concerning requested services, project locations, known safety concerns, property access, scheduling requirements, and applicable credential requirements.

Client represents that it possesses appropriate authority to request services for the identified property or project and to share the information necessary to facilitate the introduction.

Client shall independently evaluate and accept Contractor proposals and construction agreements.

6. Contractor Qualifications

Omaha Restoration may review available registration, licensing, insurance, and business documentation as part of its sourcing process.

Omaha Restoration shall not knowingly represent unverified credentials as verified.

Credential reviews reflect information reasonably available at the time of review and do not constitute a continuing guarantee of coverage, qualifications, or regulatory compliance.

Client may independently verify credentials before authorizing physical work. Required registrations, licenses, and insurance must be maintained where applicable.

7. Individual Project Authorizations

Each compensated assignment shall be established through an affirmative written Project Authorization or other written compensation confirmation.

The authorization shall identify the project, responsible paying party, applicable fees, payment triggers, deadlines, and any agreed repeat-work obligations.

Compensation must be accepted by the responsible paying party before the compensated introduction. Silence does not constitute acceptance of new compensation obligations.

8. Compensation Structures

Omaha Restoration may offer the following compensation arrangements:

Standard Hybrid Compensation: A $150 job-start fee plus ten percent (10%) of Gross Project Revenue.

Fixed Sourcing Compensation: A predetermined amount payable upon an expressly agreed sourcing event.

Custom Compensation: Individually negotiated project-specific fees.

Recurring Commercial Compensation: Separately accepted periodic sourcing or network-access fees.

No particular compensation arrangement becomes binding on Client until affirmatively accepted in writing.

9. Payment Responsibility

Each compensated assignment shall designate Client, Contractor, or both as responsible for separately agreed compensation obligations.

Any dual-funded compensation arrangement shall be disclosed to the affected parties before introduction.

Client is not responsible for a Contractor's separate payment obligations unless expressly accepted in writing.

This Agreement does not independently impose obligations upon Contractors who have not accepted those obligations.

Any waiver or modification of a Contractor's separate compensation obligations must be documented independently.

10. Gross Project Revenue

Gross Project Revenue means the total legitimate amount charged by the introduced Contractor for qualifying work associated with the referred project, including labor, materials, equipment, service charges, approved change orders, and additional authorized project work.

Gross Project Revenue excludes sales tax separately collected and remitted to the applicable taxing authority.

Ordinary operating expenses, labor costs, material costs, equipment expenses, subcontractor expenses, and business overhead shall not reduce Gross Project Revenue.

Documented bona fide refunds, credits, and canceled charges reducing the actual project price shall be reflected in the final compensation calculation.

11. Job Start

Job Start occurs when the introduced Contractor first physically commences authorized work associated with the referred project.

Where standard hybrid compensation applies, the $150 job-start fee becomes earned upon Job Start and is payable within five business days unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Preliminary conversations, inspections, and estimates do not independently constitute Job Start.

12. Project Completion

Project Completion occurs upon the earlier of substantial completion and acceptance by the authorized party, or receipt of final payment for the completed project by the Contractor.

Applicable percentage-based compensation becomes payable within five business days following Project Completion.

Delayed construction payments do not postpone an otherwise earned sourcing fee unless expressly agreed in writing.

For projects terminated after work begins, percentage compensation shall be calculated using legitimate charges for qualifying work performed and other earned project charges. Such compensation is payable within five business days after those final charges are reasonably established.

13. Fixed and Recurring Fees

Fixed fees become earned upon the sourcing event identified in the accepted Project Authorization.

Unless otherwise agreed, fixed fees are payable within five business days following the applicable event.

Recurring commercial fees require separate written acceptance establishing billing dates, service scope, and cancellation terms.

14. Project Reporting

Where Client is responsible for compensation, Client shall accurately report relevant project pricing, commencement, completion, change orders, additional qualifying work, and material payment adjustments.

Client shall report Job Start and Project Completion within two business days after becoming aware of the applicable event.

Requested compensation-related information reasonably available to Client shall be provided within five business days.

15. Revenue Verification

Omaha Restoration may reasonably request estimates, accepted proposals, invoices, change orders, and other relevant documentation necessary to verify percentage-based compensation.

Verification shall be limited to information reasonably necessary to establish compensation associated with the referred opportunity.

Client may redact unrelated confidential, personal, banking, and commercially sensitive information not necessary for verification.

This provision does not authorize unrestricted access to Client's financial records.

16. Record Retention

Client shall retain reasonably available compensation-related project records for three years following the applicable project's completion or termination.

Records shall be retained longer when required by applicable law or reasonably necessary for an existing dispute.

17. Invoicing and Payment Collection

Omaha Restoration may issue electronic invoices identifying the assignment, compensation amount, and applicable payment deadline.

Client shall identify good-faith invoice disputes in writing within five business days after receiving the invoice, where reasonably practicable.

Undisputed amounts remain payable by their applicable deadlines.

Omaha Restoration may suspend future services while undisputed compensation remains overdue and may pursue lawful collection remedies.

Collection expenses and attorney's fees may be recovered only to the extent permitted by applicable law.

No contractual late charge or interest applies unless separately accepted in writing and permitted by applicable law.

18. Underreported Compensation

If reliable supporting documentation establishes that compensation was materially underreported, the unpaid difference remains payable.

Omaha Restoration may issue a corrected invoice. Unless otherwise agreed, undisputed additional compensation is payable within five business days after receipt of the corrected invoice.

No automatic punitive multiplier or penalty shall apply.

19. Cancellations and Contractor Withdrawals

Client may cancel a sourcing request before the applicable compensation trigger occurs.

No job-start fee is owed when physical work has not commenced. A separate lawful cancellation or sourcing fee may apply only when expressly accepted in writing.

If a Contractor withdraws or becomes unavailable, Omaha Restoration may attempt to identify an alternative Contractor upon request.

Replacement availability is not guaranteed. Cancellation does not automatically extinguish compensation already earned.

20. Refunds and Adjustments

Amounts paid for services that have not become earned shall be refunded unless a separately disclosed and accepted lawful nonrefundable fee applies.

Earned sourcing compensation is not automatically refundable because of a separate construction dispute, scheduling delay, or workmanship complaint.

Nothing in this provision eliminates Client's lawful remedies for Omaha Restoration's own breach or misrepresentation.

21. Non-Circumvention

For twelve (12) months following a documented introduction, Client shall not intentionally circumvent Omaha Restoration to avoid compensation expressly agreed upon for qualifying work arising from that introduction.

This provision is limited to the introduced Contractor, identified property or project opportunity, and applicable accepted compensation obligations.

Client may communicate, negotiate, and contract directly with Contractors while complying with applicable reporting and payment obligations.

Client shall not knowingly use affiliated entities, intermediaries, representatives, or alternative contracting arrangements to conceal qualifying work or evade compensation.

This provision does not prohibit lawful competition or unrelated independently established business relationships.

22. Repeat Work and Change Orders

Qualifying repeat work performed by an introduced Contractor at the same property within twelve months following the documented introduction remains subject to the applicable compensation structure when expressly established in the accepted Project Authorization.

Where standard hybrid repeat-work compensation applies, each distinct qualifying subsequent project is subject to a new $150 job-start fee plus ten percent (10%) of its Gross Project Revenue.

Change orders forming part of the original project are included in that project's Gross Project Revenue and do not automatically generate a separate job-start fee.

Client shall report qualifying repeat work within two business days after becoming aware of it.

23. Pre-Existing Relationships

Client does not owe compensation solely because Omaha Restoration identifies a Contractor with whom Client already maintains an independently established relationship concerning the same opportunity.

Client shall disclose relevant pre-existing relationships promptly after receiving the Contractor's identity.

Omaha Restoration may request reasonable supporting documentation establishing the independently existing relationship or opportunity.

24. Insurance-Funded Projects

Omaha Restoration does not prepare, submit, negotiate, interpret, or adjust insurance claims or determine insurance settlement amounts.

Client shall not knowingly involve Omaha Restoration in fraudulent insurance billing, false statements, unlawful claim inflation, unauthorized adjusting, or other unlawful activities.

Compensation represents payment for legitimate contractor sourcing and referral services, not insurance claim recovery or settlement negotiation.

Insurance funding does not, by itself, modify an otherwise lawful and agreed compensation obligation.

Compensation prohibited by applicable law shall not be charged or collected.

25. Confidentiality and Information Protection

Each Party shall protect nonpublic customer, property, pricing, financial, contractor, and operational information obtained through the relationship.

Information may be used or disclosed when reasonably necessary to facilitate authorized services, administer compensation, obtain professional advice, comply with legal obligations, or enforce contractual rights.

Each Party shall maintain reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information involved.

26. No Unauthorized Commitments

Client shall not make unauthorized promises, warranties, representations, or contractual commitments on behalf of Omaha Restoration.

Omaha Restoration shall not make unauthorized construction commitments on behalf of Client or any Contractor.

27. Disclaimers and Limitation of Liability

Omaha Restoration does not guarantee Contractor availability, acceptance, response times, construction pricing, schedules, workmanship, project completion, insurance coverage, or construction warranties.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, neither Party shall be liable to the other for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages arising solely from a breach of this Agreement.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, Omaha Restoration's aggregate liability for ordinary contractual claims shall not exceed the greater of $500 or the sourcing compensation paid by Client during the twelve months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

These limitations do not apply to fraud, gross negligence, willful misconduct, required refunds, or liabilities that cannot lawfully be limited.

Nothing in this Agreement eliminates responsibility for Omaha Restoration's own legally actionable conduct.

28. Indemnification

To the extent permitted by applicable law, each Party agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the other Party from third-party claims and reasonable expenses to the extent caused by the indemnifying Party's own negligence, willful misconduct, material breach, or unlawful conduct.

Neither Party shall be required to indemnify the other for the other Party's own negligence or conduct for which indemnification is prohibited by applicable law.

This provision shall be interpreted consistently with applicable Nebraska restrictions on construction-related indemnification.

29. Non-Exclusivity

This Agreement is non-exclusive.

Client may obtain contractor services through other lawful sources, and Omaha Restoration may provide services to other businesses and property owners.

Neither Party guarantees minimum project volume, continuous availability, or minimum revenue.

30. Material Breach

Material breaches may include intentional concealment of qualifying project revenue, falsification of compensation records, failure to pay undisputed earned compensation, intentional circumvention, material misrepresentation, and unlawful conduct.

The non-breaching Party may pursue lawful contractual remedies, subject to applicable notice and cure requirements.

31. Duration and Termination

This Agreement remains effective until terminated by either Party upon fifteen calendar days' written notice.

Either Party may immediately suspend or terminate services in response to material fraud, unlawful conduct, serious safety concerns, or material breaches that cannot reasonably be cured.

For other material breaches, the non-breaching Party may provide written notice allowing ten business days for reasonable correction before termination.

32. Survival of Obligations

Termination does not extinguish compensation lawfully earned or obligations arising from previously accepted Project Authorizations.

Applicable payment, verification, record retention, confidentiality, non-circumvention, repeat-work, and dispute-resolution provisions survive termination for their applicable contractual duration.

33. Force Majeure

Neither Party shall be responsible for delays in performing obligations other than payment obligations caused by circumstances reasonably beyond its control.

The affected Party shall provide reasonable notice and resume performance when reasonably practicable.

This provision does not excuse payment of compensation already earned.

34. Notices

Official notices may be delivered by email, written correspondence, or another mutually accepted electronic method providing a reasonable record of delivery.

Notices to Omaha Restoration shall be directed to Murrays Empire LLC at:

murraysempirellc@gmail.com

Notices to Client shall be directed to the business contact information provided with this Agreement or subsequently updated in writing.

35. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Nebraska.

Before initiating formal proceedings, the Parties agree to make a reasonable good-faith effort to resolve disputes through written communication.

Unless urgent legal relief is necessary, the Parties shall allow fifteen calendar days following written dispute notice for attempted resolution.

Unresolved disputes shall be brought before a court of competent jurisdiction in Douglas County, Nebraska, or the applicable federal court serving that jurisdiction.

Nothing prevents either Party from pursuing lawful small-claims remedies or emergency legal relief.

36. Regulatory Compliance

Each Party shall comply with applicable federal, state, and local laws governing its own business activities.

Nothing in this Agreement authorizes unlicensed regulated work, worker misclassification, fraudulent insurance activities, unlawful referral compensation, or avoidance of required registrations.

Activities requiring additional registration, licensing, or regulatory authorization shall not proceed until applicable requirements are satisfied.

37. Assignment and Business Succession

Neither Party may transfer material contractual obligations to an unrelated third party without written consent, except through lawful business succession where the successor assumes applicable obligations.

No transfer shall extinguish previously earned compensation or accrued obligations.

38. Severability and No Waiver

If a provision is determined to be unlawful or unenforceable, remaining provisions shall remain effective to the extent permitted by law.

Failure to enforce a provision on one occasion does not permanently waive that provision or other contractual rights.

39. Entire Agreement and Order of Precedence

This Agreement, accepted Project Authorizations, and written amendments constitute the entire agreement concerning their subject matter.

A mutually accepted written amendment controls to the extent it expressly modifies this Agreement.

An accepted Project Authorization controls conflicting general terms only for the specific assignment and provisions expressly identified.

This Agreement does not independently amend a separate Contractor Partnership Agreement.

40. Amendments and No Retroactive Changes

Material amendments require affirmative written acceptance by both Parties.

Neither Party may unilaterally change previously accepted compensation obligations or retroactively modify material assignment terms.

41. Electronic Signatures and Records

The Parties consent to conducting transactions electronically.

Electronic signatures and affirmative electronic acceptance may have the same legal effect as handwritten signatures to the extent permitted by applicable law.

Omaha Restoration may retain electronic records documenting signatory information, agreement versions, acceptance timestamps, Project Authorizations, and relevant communications.

Each Party shall be provided access to or a copy of the mutually accepted Agreement.

42. Signatory Authority

The individual electronically signing this Agreement represents that they possess sufficient authority to bind the business identified in the registration information.

The signatory acknowledges having reviewed the Agreement and having had an opportunity to obtain independent legal advice.

43. Effective Date and Mutual Acceptance

Submission of this electronically signed Agreement constitutes Client's offer to enter into the commercial relationship under the stated terms.

The Agreement becomes effective and binding upon written acceptance by an authorized representative of Murrays Empire LLC.

Submission alone does not constitute automatic approval, guaranteed contractor availability, or acceptance by Omaha Restoration.

Individual compensation obligations require separate affirmative written acceptance as provided in this Agreement.

44. Business Day Definition

Business Day means Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays observed in the United States.
