OMAHA RESTORATION™ CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT OR-CPA-2026-V8

1. Parties & Agreement Purpose

This Contractor Partnership and Referral Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Murrays Empire LLC, a Nebraska limited liability company operating under the brand Omaha Restoration™ ("Company"), and the independent contractor business identified in the registration form ("Contractor").

The purpose of this Agreement is to establish the terms under which Company may introduce prospective customers, businesses, and project opportunities to Contractor in exchange for agreed referral compensation.

Submission of an application does not guarantee acceptance into the network, referrals, project awards, exclusive territories, or minimum earnings.

2. Independent Referral Platform

Omaha Restoration™ operates as an independent contractor referral and project-routing network.

Company does not directly perform restoration, mitigation, construction, plumbing, roofing, electrical, HVAC, drywall, painting, mold remediation, or other physical contracting services.

All physical services are independently estimated, contracted, managed, and performed by Contractor or another properly authorized service provider.

Contractor is solely responsible for pricing, customer contracts, labor, equipment, materials, permits, licensing, insurance, workmanship, jobsite safety, warranties, and customer satisfaction.

Nothing in this Agreement establishes employment, agency, franchise, joint venture, or legal partnership. Contractor has no authority to bind Company.

This Agreement does not authorize Company to supply employees, administer payroll, supervise workers, or operate as an employer of record. Any employment-placement arrangement requires separate review and applicable legal compliance.

3. Definitions

3.1 Referred Lead

A "Lead" means a prospective customer, property owner, property manager, business, or project opportunity introduced or transmitted by Company through telephone, text message, email, website, electronic platform, or another documented communication method.

A "Referred Customer" means a customer or business first introduced to Contractor through a documented referral from Company.

3.2 Job Start

"Job Start" means the earliest occurrence of:

  • A project-specific on-site inspection or assessment for an assignment expressly accepted under the standard compensation model.
  • Customer approval of a project estimate.
  • Execution of a work authorization or service contract.
  • Commencement of physical project work.
  • Delivery or placement of project equipment for an authorized project.

Only one Job Start fee applies to each separately identifiable referred project unless otherwise agreed in writing.

3.3 Gross Project Revenue

"Gross Project Revenue" means the total amount charged or invoiced to the customer for a referred project before deductions.

It includes:

  • Labor and professional services.
  • Materials and supplies.
  • Equipment and equipment rentals.
  • Subcontractor charges.
  • Project management charges.
  • Approved change orders.
  • Additional project services.
  • Other project-related charges.

Gross Project Revenue excludes separately stated sales taxes collected and remitted to the appropriate taxing authority.

Operating expenses, labor costs, material costs, subcontractor payments, overhead, and payment processing charges do not reduce Gross Project Revenue.

Documented refunds and credits actually issued to the customer reduce the applicable revenue calculation.

3.4 Job Completion

"Job Completion" occurs upon the earlier of:

  • Substantial completion of contracted work and customer acceptance.
  • Receipt of final payment for the project.

3.5 Business Day

A Business Day means Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays.

3.6 Assignment Confirmation

An Assignment Confirmation means a documented communication identifying the referred opportunity, applicable compensation terms, and any assignment-specific conditions accepted by Company and Contractor.

4. Required Referral Compensation

4.1 Applicable Compensation Model

Company offers two project-based compensation models:

  • Small Project Referral Compensation.
  • Standard Project Referral Compensation.

Company shall identify the applicable compensation terms in writing before Contractor accepts an assignment.

When the project value is unknown, the assignment confirmation may identify the compensation schedule as applicable according to the subsequently documented project value.

Contractor may accept or decline the assignment and its disclosed compensation terms.

Contractor may not independently substitute, reduce, or change accepted compensation terms.

Any assignment-specific modification must be documented and accepted in writing by both Parties.

Contractor is responsible for the applicable referral compensation unless Company expressly agrees otherwise in writing.

4.2 Small Project Referral Compensation

Qualifying paid referred projects with Gross Project Revenue below One Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($1,500.00) are subject to the following flat-fee schedule:

  • Greater than $0 and under $250.00: $25.00.
  • $250.00 through $499.99: $50.00.
  • $500.00 through $999.99: $100.00.
  • $1,000.00 through $1,499.99: $150.00.

Small-project compensation replaces the standard $150 Job Start fee and 10% Gross Project Revenue fee for that qualifying project.

The small-project referral fee becomes earned when the customer approves a paid project or physical paid project work begins, whichever occurs first.

The applicable fee is payable within five (5) Business Days following that event.

An unsuccessful estimate, unpaid inspection, or customer introduction alone does not trigger a small-project referral fee.

4.3 Small Project Value Adjustments

If approved change orders, additional services, or revised project charges increase the total Gross Project Revenue, the referral fee shall be adjusted to the applicable project-value tier.

If the total project value reaches or exceeds $1,500.00, the Standard Project Referral Compensation model applies unless an alternative arrangement was expressly accepted in writing.

Previously paid small-project referral fees shall be credited against the recalculated total referral compensation.

Any remaining balance is payable within five (5) Business Days following Job Completion, or within five (5) Business Days following subsequent invoicing of additional charges.

4.4 Standard Project Job Start Fee

For qualifying standard projects valued at $1,500.00 or more, Contractor agrees to pay Company a flat referral fee of One Hundred Fifty Dollars ($150.00) for each qualifying Job Start.

The Job Start fee is due within five (5) Business Days following Job Start.

The fee remains payable if the customer subsequently cancels the project, provided a qualifying Job Start actually occurred and the standard compensation model was accepted for the assignment.

Where project value is initially unknown, an inspection alone does not establish the standard Job Start fee unless that fee was expressly accepted in writing before the inspection.

4.5 Standard Gross Project Revenue Fee

Contractor agrees to pay Company ten percent (10%) of Gross Project Revenue for each qualifying standard referred project.

Payment is due within five (5) Business Days following Job Completion.

The $150 Job Start fee and 10% Gross Project Revenue fee are separate obligations.

Except for credits expressly provided under this Agreement, the $150 fee shall not be deducted from or credited against the 10% revenue fee.

4.6 Unknown Project Value

When the project value cannot reasonably be determined before assignment acceptance, Company and Contractor may document a provisional project-value classification or accept the applicable compensation schedule based on the final documented project value.

Contractor must report the quoted project amount and customer approval in accordance with Section 7.

The final referral fee shall be calculated using the actual documented Gross Project Revenue and accepted compensation terms.

No undisclosed fee or retroactive compensation model may be imposed.

4.7 Additional Project Revenue

Change orders, project expansions, and additional charges are included in Gross Project Revenue.

Additional referral fees resulting from charges invoiced after Job Completion are due within five (5) Business Days after those charges are invoiced.

4.8 Project Splitting & Fee Avoidance

Contractor shall not intentionally divide, reclassify, underreport, or separately invoice a single project for the purpose of reducing or avoiding applicable referral compensation.

Related work forming part of the same contracted project shall be evaluated using the combined applicable project value.

Legitimately separate projects may be classified independently when supported by accurate documentation.

4.9 No Duplicate Compensation

Company shall not collect both the small-project flat fee and standard project compensation as separate cumulative compensation models for the same project.

Amounts already paid toward a project shall be credited when required under the accepted compensation arrangement.

4.10 Payment Methods

All fees must be paid using an authorized business payment method designated by Company.

Payment methods may include ACH, bank transfer, approved electronic business payment services, or another method authorized in writing.

Contractor must identify the corresponding project or assignment when submitting payment.

4.11 Late Payments

Overdue, undisputed amounts may accrue a late charge of 1.5% per month, without compounding, or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law, whichever is less.

If an undisputed payment remains overdue for fifteen (15) calendar days, Company may suspend additional referrals until the balance is resolved.

Reasonable collection costs and attorney fees may be recovered only to the extent permitted by applicable law or awarded under the dispute-resolution provisions.

4.12 Payment Disputes

Contractor must provide written notice identifying a disputed fee and supporting documentation within ten (10) Business Days after receiving the invoice.

The Parties shall attempt to resolve the dispute in good faith. Undisputed amounts remain payable by their original deadlines.

5. Non-Circumvention & Repeat Customer Protection

5.1 Protected Referral Period

For twelve (12) months following Company's first documented introduction of a Referred Customer, Contractor shall not intentionally circumvent Company to avoid referral compensation on qualifying additional work arising from that introduction.

The protected period applies separately to each customer introduced by Company.

5.2 Repeat Projects

Contractor must notify Company before accepting additional qualifying work involving a Referred Customer or the originally referred property during the protected period.

Each separately contracted qualifying additional project is subject to the applicable compensation schedule under Section 4, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

A prior small-project arrangement does not establish a permanent reduced compensation arrangement for future projects.

5.3 Direct Customer Communication

Contractor may communicate directly with customers when reasonably necessary to estimate, schedule, perform, or administer authorized work.

However, Contractor may not intentionally redirect customers or structure transactions to avoid applicable referral fees.

Using business cards, telephone numbers, websites, affiliated companies, or other communication methods for the purpose of circumventing Company constitutes a material breach.

Contractor shall not knowingly use an affiliated business, subcontractor, intermediary, or separate payment arrangement to conceal qualifying work or avoid applicable referral compensation.

5.4 Unreported Work

Contractor must disclose previously unreported qualifying work within twenty-four (24) hours after discovering the omission.

Late disclosure does not eliminate fees owed or automatically cure a contractual breach.

5.5 Existing Customer Relationships

This section does not apply to independently obtained projects or customer relationships Contractor can reasonably document existed before Company's introduction.

5.6 Survival

These obligations survive expiration or termination for the remainder of the applicable twelve-month protected period.

6. Lead Acceptance & Performance

Contractor agrees to:

  • Accept or decline each referral within 24 hours.
  • Review applicable compensation before acceptance.
  • Contact the customer within 24 hours after accepting.
  • Provide accurate scheduling information.
  • Notify Company promptly of missed appointments or delays.
  • Maintain professional customer communication.
  • Provide truthful project updates.

Repeated missed appointments, failure to respond, customer abandonment, or intentional misrepresentation may result in suspension or removal from the network.

Company may reassign unanswered or declined referrals.

7. Mandatory Project Reporting

Contractor must report the following events within twenty-four (24) hours of their occurrence:

  • Initial customer contact.
  • Appointment scheduling.
  • Estimate preparation and quoted amount.
  • Customer approval or rejection.
  • Job Start date.
  • Initial contract amount.
  • Change orders and revised project value.
  • Job Completion date.
  • Final invoice amount.
  • Qualifying repeat work.

Contractor must maintain accurate project and assignment records for at least two (2) years following completion.

Upon reasonable written request, Contractor shall provide relevant estimates, contracts, invoices, change orders, and payment records necessary to verify applicable referral compensation.

Contractor shall ordinarily respond within five (5) Business Days.

Company may lawfully contact referred customers to verify project status, appointment attendance, and applicable project amounts.

Knowingly concealing revenue or falsifying project information constitutes a material breach.

8. Licensing, Registration & Insurance

Contractor must maintain all licenses, registrations, permits, qualifications, and business authority required by law for the services it accepts.

Before receiving the first independent project referral, Contractor must provide a current certificate of insurance demonstrating:

  • Commercial General Liability: $1,000,000 per occurrence.
  • Commercial General Liability: $2,000,000 general aggregate.
  • Workers' Compensation where required by applicable law.

Company may require additional coverage appropriate to specialized or higher-risk services.

Contractor must promptly notify Company if required insurance or credentials expire, are canceled, suspended, or materially change.

Company may verify credentials and suspend referrals until satisfactory documentation is provided.

8.1 Conditional Applicant Review

An applicant who does not currently maintain required registration, insurance, or professional qualifications may submit information for preliminary review.

Conditional review does not constitute approval as an independent contractor within the Omaha Restoration network.

No independent contracting assignment shall be authorized until all applicable requirements are satisfied.

Any separate opportunity involving employment by another business must be evaluated independently and is not authorized under this Agreement.

8.2 Jurisdiction-Specific Requirements

Contractor is responsible for satisfying the legal and regulatory requirements applicable to the state, municipality, and service category where work occurs.

Approval for one service or jurisdiction does not establish approval for every other service or jurisdiction.

9. Confidentiality & Brand Protection

Customer information, project inquiries, lead records, and nonpublic Company business information disclosed through the referral relationship are confidential.

Contractor may use this information only for legitimate purposes associated with the referred project, lawful recordkeeping, and related customer obligations.

Contractor may not sell, improperly distribute, or disclose confidential referral information to unauthorized third parties.

Contractor may not:

  • Claim to be an employee of Omaha Restoration™.
  • Claim authority to bind Company.
  • Make unauthorized promises on Company's behalf.
  • Use Company logos or trademarks without written permission.
  • Misrepresent its qualifications or relationship with Company.
  • Intentionally misuse referral information to avoid fees.

Applicable confidentiality obligations survive termination, subject to law and legitimate disclosure requirements.

10. Independent Contractor Liability & Indemnification

Contractor is an independent business responsible for its own personnel, subcontractors, taxes, insurance, permits, equipment, project methods, jobsite safety, workmanship, and customer contracts.

To the extent permitted by law, Contractor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Murrays Empire LLC, Omaha Restoration™, and their respective owners, members, managers, and authorized representatives against third-party claims, damages, liabilities, and reasonable expenses arising from:

  • Contractor's negligent acts or omissions.
  • Contractor's contractual breaches.
  • Contractor's unlawful conduct.
  • Contractor's personnel or subcontractors.
  • Contractor's physical work or jobsite operations.
  • Failure to obtain legally required permits or credentials.

This provision does not require indemnification for Company's own negligence, misconduct, or other liability where prohibited by law.

11. Term, Renewal & Termination

11.1 Initial Term

This Agreement has an initial term of twelve (12) months beginning upon Company's documented acceptance.

11.2 Automatic Renewal

The Agreement automatically renews for successive twelve-month periods unless terminated in accordance with this section.

11.3 Standard Termination

Either Party may terminate the Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

11.4 Immediate Termination

Company may terminate immediately upon written notice for:

  • Material contractual breach.
  • Fraud or intentional misrepresentation.
  • Nonpayment of undisputed fees.
  • Intentional referral circumvention.
  • Material insurance or licensing violations.
  • Serious customer safety violations.

Company may temporarily suspend referrals while investigating credible compliance concerns.

11.5 Continuing Obligations

Termination does not eliminate accrued referral fees or qualifying payment obligations arising from referrals made before termination.

Applicable confidentiality, recordkeeping, indemnification, dispute-resolution, and non-circumvention obligations survive termination.

12. Nebraska Law & Binding Arbitration

This Agreement is governed by Nebraska law, subject to mandatory applicable laws in other jurisdictions.

Before commencing arbitration, a Party must provide written notice describing the dispute and requested remedy.

The Parties shall attempt good-faith informal resolution for fifteen (15) calendar days.

Unresolved disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association under its applicable Commercial Arbitration Rules.

Either Party may instead bring an eligible claim in a court of competent small-claims jurisdiction, subject to applicable law.

Arbitration shall take place in Douglas County, Nebraska, unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.

Unless applicable rules require otherwise, one neutral arbitrator shall decide the dispute.

Filing fees, administrative expenses, and arbitrator compensation shall initially be allocated under applicable AAA rules.

The arbitrator may award costs and attorney fees only when authorized by applicable law or an enforceable contractual provision.

A court of competent jurisdiction may enter judgment upon the arbitration award.

13. Assignment, Notices & Entire Agreement

Contractor may not assign this Agreement or transfer referred opportunities to another business without Company's prior written consent.

Required notices may be delivered through the Parties' designated business email addresses or another documented written method.

This Agreement constitutes the complete agreement regarding the referral relationship and supersedes prior agreements concerning the same subject, except accrued obligations expressly preserved.

Existing assignments remain governed by their previously accepted compensation terms unless both Parties expressly agree to modify those terms in writing.

Amendments must be in writing and accepted by authorized representatives of both Parties.

If any provision is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue to the extent permitted by law.

Failure to enforce a provision does not automatically waive future enforcement.
