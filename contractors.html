13. Network Participation Agreement

Referral terms, payment obligations, disclosures, and liability limits.

1. Parties

This Contractor Network Participation Agreement is between Murrays Empire LLC, operating Omaha Restoration™, and the contractor business submitting this form.

2. Referral Platform Disclosure

Omaha Restoration™ is a referral and routing platform. Omaha Restoration™ does not perform restoration, mitigation, construction, mold, roofing, plumbing, HVAC, electrical, or repair work. All physical services are performed by independent third-party contractors.

3. Lead Definition

A “Lead” means any customer inquiry, contact record, form submission, phone request, email request, referral notice, project information, or other customer opportunity provided or routed by Omaha Restoration™ or Murrays Empire LLC to the Contractor.

4. Job Start Definition

“Job Start” means the first occurrence of any of the following: Contractor performs an on-site paid visit, Contractor obtains a signed work authorization, Contractor obtains a signed estimate approval, Contractor places equipment at the property, Contractor begins labor, Contractor receives a deposit, or Contractor otherwise begins compensated work connected to a Lead.

5. Job Completion Definition

“Job Completion” means the project is finished, invoiced, paid in full or in part, closed by the Contractor, submitted to insurance, or otherwise completed enough for Contractor to claim or collect compensation related to the Lead.

6. Gross Revenue Definition

“Gross Revenue” means all money invoiced, charged, claimed, collected, or received by Contractor from a customer, insurance company, property owner, tenant, adjuster, financing provider, or other party for work connected to a Lead before deductions for labor, materials, subcontractors, equipment, taxes, fees, discounts, overhead, or other expenses.

7. Required Referral Fees

The Referral Fee consists of two required components: (a) One Hundred Fifty Dollars ($150.00) due within five (5) business days of Job Start, and (b) ten percent (10%) of Gross Revenue due within five (5) business days of Job Completion.

8. Reporting Requirement

Contractor must notify Omaha Restoration™ within twenty-four (24) hours after receiving a Lead status update, including whether the customer was contacted, inspected, scheduled, declined, converted, started, completed, invoiced, or paid.

9. Records and Verification

Contractor agrees to keep accurate records for all Leads, Job Starts, invoices, payments, and Job Completions. Upon reasonable request, Contractor agrees to provide documentation needed to verify whether referral fees are owed and the amount due.

10. Late Payment

Any unpaid referral fee not paid within five (5) business days of the required due date may result in immediate suspension from future referrals. Late balances may also be subject to lawful collection efforts, reasonable collection costs, and any interest allowed by applicable law.

11. Independent Contractor Relationship

Contractor is an independent business. Nothing in this agreement creates an employment relationship, partnership, agency relationship, joint venture, franchise, or employer-employee relationship between Contractor, Omaha Restoration™, or Murrays Empire LLC.

12. Contractor Responsibility

Contractor is solely responsible for all work performed, jobsite safety, customer communications, pricing, permits, licenses, insurance, warranties, workers, subcontractors, taxes, tools, vehicles, equipment, compliance, and customer satisfaction.

13. No Guarantee of Leads

Omaha Restoration™ does not guarantee lead volume, job volume, revenue, customer conversion, project size, or payment by any customer or third party.

14. Indemnification

Contractor agrees to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Omaha Restoration™, Murrays Empire LLC, and their owners, members, agents, and representatives from claims, damages, disputes, losses, costs, liabilities, and expenses arising from Contractor’s work, conduct, omissions, employees, subcontractors, customer disputes, regulatory violations, jobsite incidents, warranties, or services performed.

15. Termination

Either party may terminate participation in the contractor network by written notice. Any referral fees owed for Leads delivered before termination remain due and collectible after termination.

16. Governing Law and Arbitration

This agreement is governed by Nebraska law. Any dispute that cannot be resolved informally shall be resolved through binding arbitration in Douglas County, Nebraska, unless applicable law requires otherwise.

17. Attorney Fees and Collection Costs

To the extent allowed by law, the prevailing party in a dispute or collection action related to this agreement may recover reasonable attorney fees, arbitration fees, court costs, and collection costs.
