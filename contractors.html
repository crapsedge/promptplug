OMAHA RESTORATION™ CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT
AGREEMENT REVIEW REQUIRED Please read the entire Omaha Restoration™ Contractor Agreement before signing and submitting your application. You must scroll to the bottom of the agreement to continue.

1. Who We Are and How We Work

This agreement is between Murrays Empire LLC, a Nebraska limited liability company doing business as Omaha Restoration™ ("we," "us," or "Company"), and the contractor business listed in this application ("you" or "Contractor").

We connect contractors with customers and job opportunities in exchange for referral fees. A referral is a customer, property owner, property manager, business, or job opportunity we introduce through a call, text, email, website, electronic platform, or other documented communication.

A referred customer is a customer or business we first introduce to you through a documented referral.

We do not perform physical restoration, construction, mitigation, plumbing, roofing, electrical, HVAC, drywall, painting, mold remediation, or other contracting work. You or another properly authorized service provider independently estimate, contract, manage, and perform the work.

You run your own business. You are responsible for your prices, customer contracts, workers, subcontractors, taxes, tools, materials, equipment, permits, insurance, licenses, work methods, safety, workmanship, warranties, and customer satisfaction.

This agreement does not make you our employee, agent, franchisee, legal partner, or joint venture partner. You cannot make agreements or promises that legally bind us.

We do not supply employees, handle payroll, supervise workers, or act as an employer of record under this agreement. Any separate employment-placement opportunity requires its own review and legal compliance.

Applying does not guarantee approval, jobs, referrals, project awards, exclusive service areas, or earnings.

2. Referral Fees and Payments

Before You Accept a Job

We will identify the job, applicable fees, and any special conditions in writing before you accept. An assignment confirmation is the documented job offer and its terms accepted by both sides.

You may accept or decline each job. You are responsible for the agreed fees unless we expressly agree otherwise in writing. Neither side may change accepted fees on its own. Both sides must agree to changes in writing.

Small Jobs

For qualifying paid jobs worth less than $1,500, you pay one flat referral fee:

  • Over $0 and under $250: $25.
  • $250–$499.99: $50.
  • $500–$999.99: $100.
  • $1,000–$1,499.99: $150.

The fee is earned when the customer approves a paid job or paid physical work begins, whichever happens first. Pay the fee within five business days after that event.

An introduction, unsuccessful estimate, or unpaid inspection alone does not trigger a small-job fee.

The small-job fee replaces the standard $150 Job Start fee and 10% fee for that job. We do not charge both compensation models as separate cumulative fees for the same job.

Standard Jobs

For qualifying jobs worth $1,500 or more, you pay:

  • $150 when the job starts.
  • 10% of the total project revenue.

The $150 fee is due within five business days after Job Start. The 10% fee is due within five business days after Job Completion.

These are separate fees. The $150 fee does not reduce the 10% fee unless a credit is expressly allowed under this agreement.

What Counts as Job Start?

Job Start is the first of these events:

  • You perform an on-site inspection or assessment for a specific job expressly accepted under the standard fee model.
  • The customer approves the estimate.
  • A work authorization or service contract is signed.
  • Physical work begins.
  • Equipment is delivered or placed for an authorized job.

Only one Job Start fee applies to each separately identifiable referred job unless both sides agree otherwise in writing.

If the customer cancels after a qualifying Job Start, the $150 fee remains due when the standard fee model was accepted.

If the job value is unknown, an inspection alone does not trigger the $150 fee unless you expressly accepted that fee in writing before the inspection.

What Does Total Project Revenue Mean?

Total project revenue, also called Gross Project Revenue, is the full amount charged or invoiced to the customer before deductions. It is not your profit.

It includes labor, professional services, materials, supplies, equipment, rentals, subcontractor charges, project management, approved change orders, extra services, and other project charges.

You cannot subtract your business expenses, labor costs, materials, subcontractor payments, overhead, or payment processing fees.

Separately listed sales taxes collected and paid to the proper tax authority are excluded. Documented refunds and credits actually given to the customer reduce the revenue amount.

When Is a Job Complete?

Job Completion happens when the contracted work is substantially complete and the customer accepts it, or when you receive final payment, whichever happens first.

Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays.

Unknown Prices and Extra Work

If the job price is unknown, both sides may agree to a temporary price category or use the fee schedule based on the final documented job value.

You must report the quoted price and customer approval under Section 4. The final fee is based on the actual documented revenue and accepted terms. We cannot impose undisclosed fees or retroactively change the fee model.

If extra work, approved changes, or revised charges increase the job value, the fee moves to the correct price tier.

If the job reaches $1,500, the standard $150 plus 10% model applies unless both sides expressly accepted different terms in writing.

Any small-job fees already paid count toward the recalculated total fee. The remaining balance is due within five business days after Job Completion or subsequent invoicing of additional charges.

Extra charges, change orders, and project expansions count toward total revenue. Additional referral fees from charges invoiced after completion are due within five business days after those charges are invoiced.

You cannot intentionally split, rename, underreport, or separately invoice one job to avoid fees. Related work under the same contracted project is counted together. Truly separate jobs may be treated separately when properly documented.

Payments already made are credited when required by the accepted fee arrangement.

Making Payments

Pay using an authorized business payment method we designate, such as ACH, bank transfer, approved electronic business payments, or another method approved in writing. Identify the job with each payment.

Undisputed overdue fees may be charged 1.5% per month without compounding, or the legal maximum if it is lower.

If an undisputed payment is more than 15 calendar days overdue, we may stop sending referrals until the balance is resolved.

Reasonable collection costs and attorney fees may be recovered only when permitted by law or awarded under the dispute resolution terms.

If you dispute an invoice, notify us in writing within 10 business days of receiving it. Identify the disputed fee and provide supporting documents. Both sides must try to resolve the issue in good faith. Undisputed amounts remain due on their original dates.

3. Protecting Our Customer Referrals

For 12 months after we first document our introduction of each referred customer, you cannot intentionally go around Omaha Restoration to avoid paying referral fees on qualifying additional work resulting from that introduction.

Before accepting qualifying additional work involving that customer or the originally referred property during the protected period, you must notify us.

Each separately contracted qualifying job follows the fee schedule unless both sides agree otherwise in writing. A reduced fee on one job does not guarantee reduced fees on future jobs.

You may communicate directly with customers to estimate, schedule, perform, and manage authorized work.

You cannot intentionally redirect customers or arrange payments to avoid our fees. You cannot use business cards, phone numbers, websites, related businesses, subcontractors, middlemen, separate payments, or other methods to intentionally hide qualifying work or avoid referral fees. Doing so is a serious breach of this agreement.

If you discover qualifying work that was not reported, tell us within 24 hours. Reporting it late does not erase fees owed or automatically fix a contract violation.

This protection does not apply to independently obtained jobs or customer relationships you can reasonably document existed before our introduction.

The protection continues for the remainder of each customer's 12-month period even if this agreement ends.

4. Your Responsibilities and Job Updates

You must:

  • Review the fees before accepting a job.
  • Accept or decline each referral within 24 hours.
  • Contact the customer within 24 hours after accepting.
  • Provide accurate appointment information.
  • Promptly report missed appointments or delays.
  • Treat customers professionally.
  • Provide honest job updates.

We may reassign unanswered or declined jobs. Repeated missed appointments, failure to respond, customer abandonment, or intentional dishonesty may result in suspension or removal.

You must report the following within 24 hours after each event happens:

  • First customer contact.
  • Appointment scheduling.
  • Estimate preparation and quoted price.
  • Customer approval or rejection.
  • Job Start date.
  • Original contract amount.
  • Change orders and revised prices.
  • Job Completion date.
  • Final invoice amount.
  • Qualifying repeat work.

Keep accurate job and assignment records for at least two years after completion.

When reasonably requested in writing, provide relevant estimates, contracts, invoices, change orders, and payment records needed to verify our fees. You should ordinarily respond within five business days.

We may lawfully contact referred customers to confirm job status, appointment attendance, and project amounts.

Knowingly hiding revenue or providing false job information is a serious breach of this agreement.

5. Insurance, Legal Requirements and Protection

Required Credentials

You must maintain all licenses, registrations, permits, qualifications, and business authority required by law for the work you accept.

Before receiving your first independent project referral, provide a current certificate of insurance showing:

  • General liability insurance of $1 million per occurrence.
  • General liability insurance of $2 million total aggregate coverage.
  • Workers' compensation insurance when legally required.

We may require additional insurance for specialized or higher-risk work.

Promptly notify us if required credentials or insurance expire, are canceled, suspended, or materially change.

We may verify your credentials and stop referrals until satisfactory proof is provided.

You may apply without current credentials for preliminary review. This does not mean you are approved. No independent contracting assignment will be authorized until all applicable requirements are met.

Any employment opportunity with another business requires separate review and is not authorized by this agreement.

You must follow the laws and requirements of every state, city, and service category where you work. Approval for one location or service does not approve you for all others.

Customer Information and Our Brand

Customer information, job inquiries, referral records, and our nonpublic business information are confidential. Use them only for legitimate referred work, lawful recordkeeping, and related customer obligations.

Do not sell, improperly share, or disclose this information to unauthorized people.

You cannot claim to be our employee, claim authority to bind us, make unauthorized promises for us, use our logos or trademarks without written permission, misrepresent your qualifications or relationship with us, or intentionally misuse referral information to avoid fees.

These confidentiality duties continue after this agreement ends, subject to law and legitimate disclosure requirements.

Responsibility for Claims

You are responsible for your own business operations, workers, subcontractors, and work.

To the extent allowed by law, you agree to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Murrays Empire LLC, Omaha Restoration™, and their owners, members, managers, and authorized representatives against third-party claims, damages, liabilities, and reasonable expenses arising from:

  • Your negligence, including acts or failures to act.
  • Your violations of this agreement.
  • Your unlawful conduct.
  • Your workers or subcontractors.
  • Your physical work or jobsite operations.
  • Your failure to obtain legally required permits or credentials.

This means you must provide the applicable legal defense and protection against covered claims arising from your business and work.

This provision does not require indemnification for our own negligence, misconduct, or other liability where prohibited by law.

6. Agreement Length, Termination and Disputes

How Long the Agreement Lasts

This agreement lasts 12 months from our documented acceptance and automatically renews for another 12 months at a time unless ended under these terms.

Either side may end the agreement with 30 days' written notice.

We may end it immediately by written notice for:

  • A serious contract violation.
  • Fraud or intentional dishonesty.
  • Failure to pay undisputed fees.
  • Intentionally bypassing our referral fees.
  • Serious insurance or licensing violations.
  • Serious customer safety violations.

We may temporarily stop referrals while investigating credible compliance concerns.

Ending this agreement does not erase fees already owed or qualifying payment obligations from referrals made before termination.

Applicable customer protection, confidentiality, recordkeeping, indemnification, and dispute resolution duties continue after termination.

Disagreements

Nebraska law governs this agreement, subject to mandatory laws that apply in other locations.

Before starting arbitration, the party raising the dispute must provide written notice explaining the problem and requested solution.

Both sides must try in good faith to resolve the dispute for 15 calendar days.

If the dispute remains unresolved, it must be settled through binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association under its applicable Commercial Arbitration Rules.

Binding arbitration means a neutral decision-maker resolves the dispute and issues a binding decision.

Either side may instead bring an eligible claim in a proper small-claims court, subject to applicable law.

Arbitration takes place in Douglas County, Nebraska, unless both sides agree to another location in writing.

One neutral arbitrator decides the dispute unless applicable rules require otherwise.

Filing fees, administrative costs, and arbitrator compensation are initially allocated under applicable arbitration rules.

The arbitrator may award costs and attorney fees only when authorized by law or an enforceable contract provision. A court with proper authority may enter judgment on the arbitration award.

Other Agreement Rules

You cannot transfer this agreement or referred job opportunities to another business without our prior written consent.

Required notices may be sent through our designated business email addresses or another documented written method.

This is the complete agreement covering our referral relationship. It replaces earlier agreements on the same subject, except accrued obligations expressly preserved.

Existing assignments keep their previously accepted fee terms unless both sides agree to changes in writing.

Changes to this agreement must be accepted in writing by authorized representatives of both sides.

If one provision cannot legally be enforced, the remaining provisions continue to the extent permitted by law.

Not enforcing a rule once does not automatically waive our right to enforce it later.
